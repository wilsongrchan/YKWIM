How to play

Two teams race to guess the same keyword from clues made of letters, not words.

  • Clue-givers see the keyword and type a few letters. Each letter costs points, and the next costs more (10, 12, 14…). Symbols cost 5.
  • The keyword's first few consonants are banned.
  • Teams of three: Clue-giver 2 sees which letters Clue-giver 1 used, and their prices start over. Teams of two: one clue.
  • Guessers see only their own team's clues, plus the theme and letter count.
  • When one team guesses it, the other gets a short last-chance timer (10 seconds by default).
Points: each team starts with a bank (200). Every letter comes straight out of it, so spend wisely. Guess the keyword to earn a reward, plus a first-to-guess bonus and a quick-guess bonus that shrinks every second. Wrong guesses cost points too. Dropping below zero means debt, or going bankrupt (an instant loss) if the host picks that.
