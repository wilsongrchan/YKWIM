How to play

Two teams race to guess one keyword from letters, not words.

Clues

  • Type letters that hint at the keyword. Shortening words saves letters.
  • A team is a guesser plus 1, 2 or 3 clue-givers, who write one after another.
  • The keyword's first few consonants are banned.
  • Don't know the word? Clue-giver 1 can tap Pass? and the other team's Clue-giver 1 decides. If they agree, everyone gets a new word.
  • Clue-giver 1 gives a clue first, then Clue-giver 2 gives theirs, seeing which letters Clue-giver 1 used. Add something new.
  • Guessers see only their team's clues.

Points

  • Each team has a bank (200). Every letter costs points, and the cost climbs with each one (10, 12, 14…) but resets for Clue-giver 2. Symbols cost 5.
  • A correct guess earns a reward plus a quick-guess bonus. If both teams get it, the first one also gets a first-to-guess bonus.
  • A wrong guess costs points; giving up is free.
  • When one team guesses, the other gets a last-chance timer.
  • Not enough players for two teams? Pick Co-op: one team plays a set number of words and gets a rating.
