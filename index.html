Points

  • Each team has a bank (200). Every letter costs points, and the cost climbs with each one (5, 8, 10, 12…) but resets for Clue-giver 2. Symbols cost 5.
  • A correct guess earns a reward plus a quick-guess bonus. If both teams get it, the faster one also gets a first-to-guess bonus.
  • You can only write a clue you can afford. A wrong guess costs points; giving up is free. If your team doesn't get the word, the letters are refunded.
  • When one team guesses or gives up, the other gets a last-chance timer. Giving up is final.
  • Not enough players for two teams? Pick Co-op: one team plays a set number of words and gets a grade. In VS, both teams get one too.

Clue rules

  • Clues are letters and symbols. The keyword itself, and its banned consonants, are not allowed.
  • No pattern clues. Symbols can't mark where the letters sit in the word. For PANDA (P and N are banned), *A* or A*DA would be rejected, because they line up with the start or end of the word.

Winning

  • VS: play to a target score or a set number of words. Most points wins, and a tie plays one more word.
  • Co-op: one team plays a set number of words and gets a grade.

Grades

At the end, every team gets a grade, from M up to YKWIM. The more points you gain over your starting points, the better the grade. In VS the winner is still whoever has more points.

The bar beside the game shows your team's points and what you could still win.

Hover over (or tap) a part of the real bar to see its name.
