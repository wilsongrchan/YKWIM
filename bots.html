The bots use only what their seat can see. Clue-givers pick an association from a table (and squeeze or shorten it to save letters), clue-giver 2 reads the letters giver 1 used and goes for a different angle. Guessers match clues against what they know of each word. They have no real sense of meaning, so judge pacing and scoring from this, not how clever the clues are.
